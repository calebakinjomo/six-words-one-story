## The story

> **Grandma's recipe died with her. Almost.**

Six words. A whole life — a grandmother, her cooking, a family tradition —
lost with her death, and then a single word, *Almost*, flips everything:
the recipe survived. Who saved it, and how, is left unsaid.

## How it works

`main.py` is a small deterministic program (Python 3.13, no network, no
clock, no randomness, no environment reads) that writes the story to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The output is exactly six words, 39 characters plus a trailing newline —
well inside the 1–60 character gate.
